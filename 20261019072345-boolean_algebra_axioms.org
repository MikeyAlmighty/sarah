#+title: Boolean Algebra Axioms

* Boolean Algebra Axioms

*** Addition alone

| B1 | $(x+y)+z = x+(y+z)$ | associativity |
| B2 | $x+y = y+x$         | commutativity |
| B3 | $x+0 = x$           | identity      |

*** Multiplication alone

| B4 | $(x\cdot y)\cdot z = x\cdot(y\cdot z)$ | associativity |
| B5 | $x\cdot y = y\cdot x$                  | commutativity |
| B6 | $x\cdot 1 = x$                         | identity      |

*** Interaction (distributivity)

| B7 | $x\cdot(y+z) = x\cdot y + x\cdot z$ | familiar from school algebra      |
| B8 | $x+(y\cdot z) = (x+y)\cdot(x+z)$    | odd-looking: + distributes over · |

*** Complementation

| B9  | $x + \overline{x} = 1$     |
| B10 | $x \cdot \overline{x} = 0$ |
