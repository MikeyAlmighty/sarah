#+title: Well-Formed Formulae (WFF)


* Well-Formed Formulae (WFF)

In *mathematical logic, propositional logic, and predicate logic*, a ~well-formed formula, abbreviated WFF or wff~, often simply ~formula~, is a finite sequence of symbols from a given alphabet, constructed following the defined grammar of a formal language.

** Rules for wff

- *Rule 1:* All atoms are wff.
- *Rule 2:* If $X$ and $Y$ are wff, then so are:
  - $(\neg X)$
  - $(X \land Y)$
  - $(X \lor Y)$
  - $(X \oplus Y)$
  - $(X \leftrightarrow Y)$
  - $(X \to Y)$
- *Rule 3:* All wff are built by applying Rules 1 and 2 a *finite* number of times.
