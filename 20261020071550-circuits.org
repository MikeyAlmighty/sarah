#+title: Circuits

* Circuits

** Overview

Diagrams built from [[id:650c84d1-3222-477e-9dd7-d130ba9a2bc7][Gates]] are called *circuits*.

They show how *Boolean functions* can be computed.

Mathematical *circuits* can be turned into /physical/ ones by building *gates* from simpler elements called *transistors*, which operate like electronic switches.
