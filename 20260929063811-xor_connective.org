#+title: XOR Connective

* XOR Connective

- aka *Exclusive Disjunction*.

** Definition

The connective *xor* (written $p \oplus q$) is a *binary connective* corresponding to the /exclusive/ use of "or" in everyday speech.

The compound statement "$p$ xor $q$" is true when /exactly one/ of $p$, $q$ is ~true~.

** Truth table

| $p$ | $q$ | $p \oplus q$ |
|-----+-----+--------------|
| T   | T   | F            |
| T   | F   | T            |
| F   | T   | T            |
| F   | F   | F            |

$p \oplus q$ is ~false~ if /both/ $p$ and $q$ are ~true~ (and also if both are ~false~).

** Notes

- Commutative: $p \oplus q \equiv q \oplus p$.
- Differs from [[id:74a565ae-9ce8-4967-9896-7409a6860ebb][OR]] only on the row T, T, where or gives T and xor gives F.
- Introduced to resolve the ambiguity of English "or": *or* is inclusive, *xor* is exclusive.
