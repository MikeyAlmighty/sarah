#+title: Connective Notation Summary


* Connective Notation Summary

** Overview

The six connectives of the [[id:dc24ea52-d78b-4974-9768-5199afb26eee][Propositional Logic (PL)]].

Their truth tables are the "rules" of the language, so we replace the English words with symbols.

The combined truth table below *defines* the meaning of the connectives.

** Notation

| English | Symbol            | Name                  | Note               |
|---------+-------------------+-----------------------+--------------------|
| not     | $\lnot$           | negation              | [[id:4c9f66d0-82fd-4508-a8bb-37f72813e9d0][Not Connective]]     |
| and     | $\land$           | conjunction           | [[id:dfeac7cd-c703-4665-8ba5-f6e863306ce8][And Connective]]     |
| or      | $\lor$            | disjunction           | [[id:74a565ae-9ce8-4967-9896-7409a6860ebb][OR Connective]]      |
| xor     | $\oplus$          | exclusive disjunction | [[id:791af3eb-ea5c-4458-85c8-9e7c93edf32f][XOR Connective]]     |
| iff     | $\leftrightarrow$ | biconditional         | [[id:1669b6bc-818a-4ffa-a1ef-6e731927644b][IFF Connective]]     |
| implies | $\to$             | implication           | [[id:e121cbaf-71cd-4572-be24-3f443145bcee][Implies Connective]] |

** Combined truth table

| $p$ | $q$ | $\lnot p$ | $p \land q$ | $p \lor q$ | $p \oplus q$ | $p \leftrightarrow q$ | $p \to q$ |
|-----+-----+-----------+-------------+------------+--------------+-----------------------+-----------|
| T   | T   | F         | T           | T          | F            | T                     | T         |
| T   | F   | F         | F           | T          | T            | F                     | F         |
| F   | T   | T         | F           | T          | T            | F                     | T         |
| F   | F   | T         | F           | F          | F            | T                     | T         |

** Notes

- *Unary*: $\lnot$. *Binary*: $\land, \lor, \oplus, \leftrightarrow, \to$.
- Only $\to$ is not commutative.
- Bracket placement matters: $\lnot(p \leftrightarrow q)$ differs from $\lnot p \leftrightarrow q$.
- $\lnot(p \leftrightarrow q)$ is logically equivalent to $p \oplus q$.
- Inclusive vs exclusive: $\lor$ and $\oplus$ differ only on the T, T row.
