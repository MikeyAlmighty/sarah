#+title: Homogeneous

* Homogeneous

** Definition

*Homogeneous* means consisting of the same type.

In Java a *homogeneous* *collection* stores objects of only *one* *specific* *type*.

*** Example

#+begin_src java
ArrayList dogs = new ArrayList<>();

dogs.add(new Dog());
dogs.add(new Dog());
#+end_src

Here, the [[id:0b9a2ac7-77ee-4d0c-9ed7-a262b911d3a3][ArrayList]] is *homogeneous* because it *only* contains ~Dog~ objects.

*** Why it matters
- Ensures *type* *safety*
- No need for *casting*
- Easier to reason about data

*** Contrast

- *Homogeneous*: *same* *type* only
- *[[id:02899425-122e-473b-a920-8726da830404][Heterogeneous]]*: *multiple* *types*

*** Key Insight

*Homogeneous* *collections*:
- Restrict data to a *single* *type*.
- Are simpler and safer.
- Do not rely on [[id:ecaa3f5f-3cd3-4ad3-93dc-1da93d3cf395][polymorphis]] for *variation*.

*** Summary

*Homogeneous* = *same* *object* *types* stored together in a *collection*.
