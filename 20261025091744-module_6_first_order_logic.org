* Module 6 First-Order Logic

** Key learnings for this module:

- Understand the *syntax* ("*grammatical rules*") of ~FOL~, including the role of *quantifiers* and *variables*.
- Understand the *semantics* (*meaning*) of ~FOL~, including the role of *quantifiers* and *variables*.
- Learn rules to construct formulae in ~FOL~.
- Explore the expressive power of ~FOL~ compared to ~PL~.

** Key concepts for this module:

- *First-order languages*:
  - *Predicates*
  - *Variables*
  - *Constants*
  - *Atomic Formulae*
  - *Quantifiers*
- *Parse trees* for formulae in ~FOL~
- Sentences in ~FOL~, *scope of a quantifier*, *bound and free variables*.
- *Structures* and *interpretations* of ~FOL~ formulas.
- *Logical equivalence* in ~FOL~ and /De Morgan’s laws/ for *quantifiers*.
