** Theorem 5.2: Nor-gates and nand-gates

The Boolean algebra version of Proposition 4.4.

1. Every *Boolean function* \(f : \mathbb{B}^m \to \mathbb{B}\) can be constructed from only *nor-gates*.
2. Every *Boolean function* \(f : \mathbb{B}^m \to \mathbb{B}\) can be constructed from only *nand-gates*.
