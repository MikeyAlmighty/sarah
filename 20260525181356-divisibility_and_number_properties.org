#+title: Divisibility and Number Properties

* Divisibility and Number Properties

The study of the *integers* and their properties is a branch of mathematics called [[id:1d1ec2ba-c268-458d-96bf-6601fc761e8d][Number Theory]].

Key concepts in *number theory*:
- *divisibility*
- *prime numbers*

This extends to modular arithmetic, congruence relations, and even cryptography. 

~Number theory~ is typically a very abstract area of mathematics, but it has many practical applications.

(I.e. *cryptography* is crucial for internet security and online banking.)

- ~Number theory~ also has many other applications in computer science, as we will see.
