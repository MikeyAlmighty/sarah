#+title: Adequate Connectives Cheat Sheet

* NAND (p | q ≡ ¬(p ∧ q)) cheat sheet

** Core constructions

| Operator | NAND-only form  | Why               |
|----------+-----------------+-------------------|
| ¬p       | p ∣ p            | p ∧ p ≡ p         |
| p ∧ q    | (p ∣ q) ∣ (p ∣ q) | negate the NAND   |
| p ∨ q    | (p ∣ p) ∣ (q ∣ q) | De Morgan         |
| p → q    | p ∣ (q ∣ q)      | p → q ≡ ¬(p ∧ ¬q) |
** Method

1. Rewrite the *wff* using only ¬ and ∧ (or ¬ and ∨)
2. Replace ¬x with x | x
3. Replace x ∧ y with (x | y) | (x | y)
4. Check with a truth table

** Identities to know

- Double negation: ¬¬p ≡ p
- De Morgan: ¬(p ∧ q) ≡ ¬p ∨ ¬q,  ¬(p ∨ q) ≡ ¬p ∧ ¬q
- Idempotence: p ∧ p ≡ p,  p ∨ p ≡ p
- Implication: p → q ≡ ¬p ∨ q

** NOR (p ↓ q ≡ ¬(p ∨ q)) is the dual
- ¬p ≡ p ↓ p
- p ∨ q ≡ (p ↓ q) ↓ (p ↓ q)
- p ∧ q ≡ (p ↓ p) ↓ (q ↓ q)

** Key fact

[[id:613e95dc-54f9-4e2a-8c23-6a24c380709c][NAND]] and [[id:5378dcd2-0f92-4714-8a07-c83f4c4cf08b][NOR]] are each functionally complete: /any/ *connective* can be built from either alone.
