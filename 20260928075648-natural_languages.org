#+title: Natural Languages

* Natural Languages

- Languages people use to communicate: English, Polish, French, Urdu, etc.
- Children acquire their "mother tongue" easily; learning another one later takes effort.
- Difficulties when learning a new one:
  - complicated grammatical rules
  - difficult pronunciation
  - *ambiguity* (the biggest one)

*** Ambiguity

A word's meaning depends on context and usage. Example: "reading"
| Sentence                                         | Use of "reading"                      |
|--------------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------|
| Alice was reading a book.                        | verb                                  |
| The assigned reading for this week is Chapter 2. | noun                                  |
| Bob lives in Reading.                            | place (a town in the south of the UK) |

- Native speakers resolve this automatically from context.
- Harder for learners, and harder still for machines.
