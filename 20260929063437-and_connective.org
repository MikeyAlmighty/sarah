#+title: And Connective

* And Connective

** Definition
The connective *and* (conjunction, written $p \land q$) is a *binary connective*: it takes two statements $p$ and $q$ and forms the *compound statement* "$p$ and $q$".

Motivating example: "Alice likes cheese and Bob likes tomatoes".
- If at least one part is false, the compound statement is false.
- If both parts are true, the compound statement is true.

** Truth table
| $p$ | $q$ | $p \land q$ |
|-----+-----+-------------|
| T   | T   | T           |
| T   | F   | F           |
| F   | T   | F           |
| F   | F   | F           |

$p \land q$ is true iff /both/ $p$ and $q$ are true.

** Notes
- The truth table fully defines the meaning of the connective.
- Commutative: $p \land q \equiv q \land p$.
