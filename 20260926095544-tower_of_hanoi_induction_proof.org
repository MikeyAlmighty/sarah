#+title: Tower of Hanoi Induction Proof

* Tower of Hanoi Induction Proof
** Recursion

$$T(n) = 2T(n-1) + 1, \quad T(1) = 1$$

Closed form to prove: $T(n) = 2^n - 1$

** Proof by Induction

*** Step 1: Base case
$n = 1$:

$$T(1) = 2^1 - 1 = 1 \checkmark$$

*** Step 2: Inductive hypothesis

Assume $T(k) = 2^k - 1$

*Target:* show that $T(k+1) = 2^{k+1} - 1$

*** Step 3: Inductive step

$$T(k+1) = 2T(k) + 1$$

$$= 2(2^k - 1) + 1$$

$$= 2 \cdot 2^k - 2 + 1$$

$$= 2^{k+1} - 1$$

So $T(k+1) = 2^{k+1} - 1$, as required.

*** Conclusion
By induction, $T(n) = 2^n - 1$ for all $n \geq 1$.
