#+title: Direct Proof

* Direct Proof

A *direct proof* is a straightforward method of proving a statement by assuming the hypothesis and logically deducing the conclusion.

* Method
1. Assume the hypothesis is true.
2. Use definitions, axioms, and previously established results.
3. Logically deduce the conclusion.

* Example

Prove that if $n$ is an even integer, then $n^2$ is also even.

** Proof
Let $n$ be an even integer. Then $n = 2k$ for some integer $k$. Therefore,

\begin{equation}
n^2 = (2k)^2 = 4k^2 = 2(2k^2)
\end{equation}

Since $2k^2$ is an integer, $n^2$ is even. $\blacksquare$
