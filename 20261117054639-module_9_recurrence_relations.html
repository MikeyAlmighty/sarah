Module 9 Recurrence Relations

Module 9 - Recurrence Relations

Key learnings for this module:

  • Understand what recurrence relations are and how they define recursive processes.
  • Learn techniques for solving recurrence relations, specifically:
    • linear homogeneous recurrence relations
    • linear inhomogeneous recurrence relations
  • Appreciate the role of recurrence relations in computer science:
    • Describing problems that break into smaller subproblems
    • Analysing recursive functions in sorting and searching algorithms
    • Predicting computational complexity and optimising performance
    • Designing recursive programs

Key concepts for this module:

  • Recurrence relations:
    • Recursive processes: problems defined in terms of smaller subproblems
    • Solution techniques for finding closed-form expressions
  • Linear recurrence relations:
    • Homogeneous
    • Inhomogeneous
  • Applications in computer science:
    • Sorting and searching algorithms
    • Complexity analysis and optimisation
    • Data structures and combinatorial counting problems
    • Recursive program design
