#+title: Connectives

* Connectives

** Description

- Ways of forming new statements from [[id:1658d69f-1ee5-4ddd-bda3-f38cf1ab6d7f][Atoms]].
- [[id:ee78ddbd-6afe-4269-8fb1-a28952bed941][Natural Language]] is the motivation and guide.

*** Example
Atoms: "Alice likes cheese" (\(p\)), "Bob likes tomatoes" (\(q\)).

| English                                     | PL              |
|---------------------------------------------+-----------------|
| Alice likes cheese *and* Bob likes tomatoes | \(p\) and \(q\) |
| Alice does *not* like cheese                | not \(p\)       |

- *and*: combines two statements (conjunction).
- *not*: changes a statement to its opposite (negation).
- Both are *connectives*.
