* Module 8 - Set Theory 2

** Key learnings for this module:

- Understand the fundamental concepts of *functions* and their *properties*:
  - /injectivity/
  - /surjectivity/
  - /bijectivity/
- Learn *combinatorial techniques*: /permutations/ and /combinations/.
- Apply these techniques to solve *counting problems*.
- Appreciate the role of *functions* and *combinatorics* in computer science:
  - Defining mappings in Databases and Data Structures
  - Designing Recursive Algorithms
  - Analysing Complexity and Optimisation

** Key concepts for this module:

- *Functions*:
  - *Properties*:
    - Injective
    - Surjective
    - Bijective
  - *Mappings* in databases and data structures (e.g. /hash tables/)
  - *Recursive* algorithms
- *Combinatorics*:
  - *Permutations*
  - *Combinations*
  - *Counting problems*
- *Applications* in computer science:
  - *Algorithm design* (e.g. counting paths in graphs)
  - *Complexity analysis* and *optimisation*
  - *Cryptography*
