* Module 7 Set Theory 1

** Key learnings for this module:

- Understand the fundamental concepts of *set theory*: *sets* and *set operations* (/union/, /intersection/, /complement/).
- Learn how to *represent sets* on a computer.
- Understand the notion of a *relation* and how to represent relations as *directed graphs*.
- Explore the *properties of relations*: /reflexivity/, /symmetry/ and /transitivity/.
- Appreciate the role of *sets* and *relations* in computer science:
  - Formalising reasoning about Computation
  - Structuring Data
  - Designing efficient Algorithms

** Key concepts for this module:

- *Sets*:
  - *Set operations*:
    - Union
    - Intersection
    - Complement
  - *Computer representation* of sets
- *Relations*:
  - *Directed graph* representation
  - *Properties*:
    - Reflexive
    - Symmetric
    - Transitive
- *Applications* in computer science:
  - Modelling *networks*, *graphs* and *databases*
  - *Search algorithms*, *hashing* and *graph search*
