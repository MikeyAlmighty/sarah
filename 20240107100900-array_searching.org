* Array Searching

*Searching* is the process of looking for a specific element in an array.

Many =algorithms= and =data structures= are devoted to *searching*.

If an ~array~ is [[id:1a328fa4-9b9f-4856-88f3-fd490bce565b][sorted]], [[id:56a130ac-4450-463f-80bd-f8361498e863][binary Search]] is more efficient than [[id:045a0561-49b8-4f28-973c-e0f840a4004f][linear search]] for finding an *element* in the ~array~.
