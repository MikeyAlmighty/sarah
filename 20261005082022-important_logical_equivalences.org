#+title: Important Logical Equivalences

* Important Logical Equivalences

** Idea

Some logical equivalences are useful for reducing a wff to a simpler but logically equivalent wff.

All of them can be proved using truth tables.

** Theorem 4.1

| Law              | Equivalence                                                                                                              |
|------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Double negation  | \(\neg\neg p \equiv p\)                                                                                                  |
| Idempotence      | \(p \land p \equiv p\) and \(p \lor p \equiv p\)                                                                         |
| Associativity    | \((p \land q) \land r \equiv p \land (q \land r)\) and \((p \lor q) \lor r \equiv p \lor (q \lor r)\)                    |
| Commutativity    | \(p \land q \equiv q \land p\) and \(p \lor q \equiv q \lor p\)                                                          |
| Distributivity   | \(p \land (q \lor r) \equiv (p \land q) \lor (p \land r)\) and \(p \lor (q \land r) \equiv (p \lor q) \land (p \lor r)\) |
| De Morgan's laws | \(\neg(p \land q) \equiv \neg p \lor \neg q\) and \(\neg(p \lor q) \equiv \neg p \land \neg q\)                          |
| Absorption       | \(p \lor (p \land q) \equiv p\) and \(p \land (p \lor q) \equiv p\)                                                      |

** Important consequences of Theorem 4.1

- *Idempotence:* \(p \land p \equiv p\) and \(p \lor p \equiv p\) mean we can "simplify" expressions that have "repetitions" of atoms.
- *Associativity:* \((p \land q) \land r \equiv p \land (q \land r)\) means we can write simply \(p \land q \land r\) without ambiguity, since both bracketings give the same truth table. Similarly, we can write \(p \lor q \lor r\) without worrying about omitting brackets.
- *Commutativity:* the order in which we carry out a sequence of conjunctions does not matter. Similarly for a sequence of disjunctions.
- *Exclusive or:* commutativity and associativity also hold for \(\oplus\):
  \[ p \oplus q \equiv q \oplus p \qquad\text{and}\qquad p \oplus (q \oplus r) \equiv (p \oplus q) \oplus r. \]
