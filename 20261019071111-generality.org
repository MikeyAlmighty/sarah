#+title: Generality

* Generality

A theory is /general/ when it is stated purely in terms of structure (a set, operations, axioms) and not in terms of what the elements are.

Every theorem proved from the axioms then holds in every instance (propositional logic, power sets, $\mathbb{B}$).
