#+title: Parse Trees

* Parse Trees

A *parse tree* will always consist of:
- vertices (circles)
- edges (lines joining two vertices).

In a *parse tree* for a *wff*, the *vertices* of the tree will always be labelled either by an *atom* or a *connective*.

The *vertices* are arranged in /horizontal/ levels.

The *vertex* at the top level is called the *root* of the parse tree.
