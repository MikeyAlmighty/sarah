#+title: Logics

* Logics

** What is a logic?

A *logic* is a formal artificial language with precise symbols, syntax (what counts as a well-formed sentence), and rules for determining whether a sentence is ~true~ or ~false~.

Different logics exist for different purposes, just as there are different programming languages.
