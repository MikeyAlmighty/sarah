#+title: Understanding Streams

* Understanding Streams

*Streams* in Java represent a /continuous/ flow of data.

** Input Streams

Used for ~reading~ data from a =source=.

** Output Streams

Used for ~writing~ data to a =destination=.


** Types of Streams

*** Byte streams

Handle *raw binary data*.

- ~InputStream~ and ~OutputStream~ classes and their =subclasses= like ~FileInputStream~ and ~FileOutputStream~.

*** Character  streams

Handle *character data*.

- ~Reader~ and ~Writer~ for better handling of *text data*, including ~BufferedReader~ and ~PrintWriter~.
