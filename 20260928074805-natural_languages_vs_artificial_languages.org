#+title: Natural Languages vs Artificial Languages

* Natural Languages vs Artificial Languages

** [[id:ee78ddbd-6afe-4269-8fb1-a28952bed941][Natural Languages]]
** Artificial languages

- In CS and maths, statements must be *unambiguous*.
- *Programming languages*: unambiguous instructions for a computer.
- *[[id:24c1bc1a-e788-4bbd-a523-84d1555fa06f][Logics]]*: symbols and rules that let mathematicians decide whether a grammatically correct sentence is ~true~ or ~false~.
- Just as there are /many/ programming languages, there are /many/ logics.

*** Logics in this course
- [[id:dc24ea52-d78b-4974-9768-5199afb26eee][Propositional Logic (PL)]]
- [[id:70421ec7-cd5b-4449-87f5-abe04c4129de][First-Order Logic (FOL)]]

Start with ~PL~: it is simpler, and ~FOL~ expands upon ~PL~.

** Comparison
| Aspect         | Natural            | Artificial                         |
|----------------+--------------------+------------------------------------|
| Origin         | evolves organically| deliberately designed              |
| Ambiguity      | common             | eliminated by design               |
| Context        | needed to interpret| not needed                         |
| Examples       | English, Urdu      | programming languages, PL, FOL     |
| Machine-friendly | hard             | easy                               |
