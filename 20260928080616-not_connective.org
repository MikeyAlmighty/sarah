#+title: Not Connective

* Not Connective (Negation)

- Given an atom \(p\), we can form the statement *not* \(p\).

** Example
- "Alice likes cheese" *true* → "Alice does *not* like cheese" *false*.
- "Alice likes cheese" *false* → "Alice does *not* like cheese" *true*.

*** Truth table

| \(p\) | not \(p\) |
|-------+-----------|
| T     | F         |
| F     | T         |
