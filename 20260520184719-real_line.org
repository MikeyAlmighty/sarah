#+title: Real Line

* Real Line

Although rational numbers appear to be very densely packed along the real line, there are still many "gaps".

There are, in fact, lengths that can be physically realised but that do not correspond to any [[id:f61bc0c5-d455-445c-bda2-c34eda3a7f7d][rational number]]. 

A *real number* that is *not* /rational/ is called [[id:cd192937-7fa2-49c5-a9fa-7d87950c1fd3][irrational]].
