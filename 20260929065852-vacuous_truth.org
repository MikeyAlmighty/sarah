#+title: Vacuous Truth

* Vacuous Truth

A *vacuous truth* is a logical statement that is /technically/ ~true~ only because the starting condition is ~false~ or impossible.

- The word "vacuous" means empty.

A *vacuous truth* is considered "empty" because it tells you nothing useful about the real world—it is ~true~ simply by default, because it cannot be proven wrong.
