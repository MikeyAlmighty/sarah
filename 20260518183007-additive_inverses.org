#+title: Additive Inverses

* Additive Inverses

Formal way of saying “the number that cancels another number out when you add them.”

If you have a number =a=, its *additive inverse* is the number that makes *zero* when /added/ to it.

So we define:

~a+(−a) = 0~

That number ~−a~ is called the *additive inverse* of ~a~.
