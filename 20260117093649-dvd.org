#+title: DVD

~DVD~ *Digital Versatile Disc* or *Digital Video Disc*.

~DVDs~ and [[id:acc7004e-2769-4dc2-9cdf-889c4678f078][CDs]] look alike, and you can use either to *store* data.

A DVD can hold *more* information than a ~CD~; a standard ~DVD’s~ storage capacity is *4.7 GB*.
There are two types of DVDs:

- ~DVD-R (Recordable)~
- ~DVD-RW (ReWritable)~
